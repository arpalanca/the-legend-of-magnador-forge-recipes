# The Legend of Magnador: Forge Recipes

An interactive forging guide for the Warcraft III RPG map **The Legend of Magnador** by Hamster_Man and BlueEagle.

**▶ Open the guide:** https://arpalanca.github.io/the-legend-of-magnador-forge-recipes/

## Why other recipe lists don't match your game

Magnador shuffles its forge recipes every time a new game starts. The map picks a random **seed** from 0 to 9, and that seed decides which two ingredients each recipe needs. A fixed list is only right if your game happened to roll the same seed, which is a 1-in-10 chance.

For example, Wooden Armor needs different ingredients depending on the seed:

| Seed | Unprocessed Armor + … |
|---|---|
| 0 | Demon Blood + Runestone |
| 3 | Magic Scroll + Stardust |
| 6 | Voodoo Doll + Silvershine Herbs |
| 9 | Bat Wing + Bones |

This guide works out your seed and then shows the correct ingredients for all 60 recipes.

## How to use the guide

1. **Find your seed** in one of these ways:
   - **From a Cookbook:** buy *Wooden Dreams* (1000 gold) or *Blood Bowl* (1500 gold) from Zardox, use it, and pick the page it shows and the first ingredient it lists.
   - **From an item you've forged:** pick the item and the two ingredients you used.
   - **If you already know it:** tap the number.
2. **Read your recipes.** Every recipe now shows the right base item and ingredients for your game.
3. **Tick the ingredients you're holding** to see what you can make right now.
4. **Tick items as you forge them.** Each recipe works only once per game.

The guide remembers your seed and checkmarks in your browser. Press **Start a new game** when you begin a new playthrough.

## Forging basics

- Learn forging from **Rondolf** first. Until then, the Mini-Forge drops any items you give it.
- Buy unprocessed bases (Helmet, Weapon, Shield, Staff, Gloves, Armor) from the **Blacksmith**.
- Ingredients drop at random from monsters.
- Give the base and both ingredients to the **Mini-Forge**.
- **Each recipe can be forged only once per game.** If you need the same effect twice, look for a twin item. For example, Ice Helm has the same 15 damage-per-second burn as Gloves of Ignition.

## Known map bug

Hands of Frost and Searing Gloves use the same ingredients. The first time you forge that combination you get Hands of Frost, and the second time you get Searing Gloves.

## Bonus: unlocking the secret hero BlueEagle

1. Pick up the **Red Orb**. It lies in the wild south of Feria, in a small round patch of dark ground with a curved pond to its east.
2. **Before** returning to Gorwin, bring it to the corpse at the north edge of Feria. **Ethan** must be carrying the orb and standing right beside the corpse.
3. Type `ARISE` in chat.

Gorwin's cutscene deletes the Red Orb, so if you've already handed it to him, you can't unlock BlueEagle in that playthrough.

## How the data was made

All recipes, item stats and mechanics were read directly from the map's own trigger script and object data (map version *Finn's Magnador 2.0 FINAL*). Nothing was copied from older guides.
