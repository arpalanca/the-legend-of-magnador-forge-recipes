How forging works

  1. Learn forging from Rondolf. Until then the Mini-Forge drops what you give it and says “Forging not learned yet.”
  2. Buy an unprocessed base from the Blacksmith: Helmet, Weapon, Shield, Staff, Gloves or Armor.
  3. Collect the two ingredients. They drop at random from monsters.
  4. Give the base and both ingredients to the Mini-Forge. It forges as soon as it holds a matching set of three.

Each recipe works once per game. Tick items off below as you forge them.

Cookbooks are sold by Zardox: Wooden Dreams (1000 gold) and Blood Bowl (1500 gold). Each use turns to the next of 3 pages.
